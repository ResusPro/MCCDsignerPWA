MCCDSigner PWA v0.8.0

Main release.

Uses the validated v0.7.0 detector/orientation/Medical Examiner box engine with the v0.8 workflow.

Desktop:
- remembered Incoming / Signed / Archive folders
- signed and archive files are written and SHA-256 verified before the Incoming original is removed
- duplicate Incoming-list entries are suppressed

Android:
- open an individual PDF using the system document picker
- review and sign locally
- share the signed PDF back to OneDrive or another approved destination

Signing:
- saved local clinician profile and transparent PNG signature
- per-user filename initials
- signed filename format: <original>-<initials>signed.pdf
- no additional watermark
- 90°/270° detected Medical Examiner boxes are automatically widened when necessary
