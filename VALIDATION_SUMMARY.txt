Safety behaviour
----------------
- Final output requires signer name, qualifications, GMC number and a PNG signature.
- JPEG signatures are rejected for final output because an opaque background can obscure certificate text.
- Desktop workflow writes and SHA-256 verifies the signed PDF and archived original before removing the original from Incoming.
- No patient PDFs are included in this public repository.
